Coverage: how should it look?

Three ways to show what the goalie covers from the Puck. Flip between them with the black bar at the bottom (or ← →). All three use the same numbers, computed live from the posed goalie with the Puck's size taken into account. Question: which shows the coverage most clearly, in Shooter's view and in the other views, and does it stay smooth while you drag the Puck and the hands?

Things to judge

  • First glance: which variant tells a young goalie "here's where you can still be beaten" fastest?
  • Views: try every view button. Where does each variant stop telling the story? (In Top-down the net face is edge-on.)
  • Shot Cone: the whole cone (A), only the lanes into the Open Net (B), or just its edges (C)?
  • The number: "87% covered" (A, C) or "13% open, here's where" (B)? Is a per-hole breakdown useful?
  • Puck size: is the dashed inner rectangle (where the Puck's centre must go to score) helpful or noise?
  • Smoothness: drag the Puck, press Sweep, and drag a hand. Watch the ms readout bottom-left.
